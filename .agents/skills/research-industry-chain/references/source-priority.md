# Source Priority

Use this reference when a bottleneck claim depends on BOM, lead time, market share, capacity, or financial validation.

## Evidence Priority By Data Type

| Data Needed | Preferred Sources | Do Not Treat As Sufficient |
|---|---|---|
| BOM or value share | teardown reports with cited method, customer spec sheets, prospectuses, industry association data, company filings | generic web diagrams, model summaries, concept-board descriptions |
| lead time | supplier/customer disclosures, order backlog, earnings calls, channel checks from reputable sources, distributor lead-time tables | old forum posts, single unsourced social post |
| capacity rigidity | capex cycle, equipment delivery, permits, yield ramp, customer certification, disclosed expansion plan | "shortage" headline without capacity path |
| market share | prospectus, annual report, industry association, reputable data vendor, named sell-side source | market-share numbers with no denominator or year |
| margin/profit pool | segment gross margin, operating margin, ASP spread, product mix disclosure | company-level margin when segment is tiny |
| pure-play exposure | segment revenue, order disclosure, product certification, named customers, official IR answer | concept-board membership or vague product keywords |
| working capital | annual/interim/quarterly reports, cash-flow statement, receivables and inventory notes | revenue growth alone |

## Search Pattern

Build searches around the exact node, not the theme:

- `{node} market share company`
- `{node} lead time shortage capacity expansion`
- `{node} BOM cost share`
- `{node} gross margin ASP price increase`
- `{company} {node} revenue customer certification capacity`
- Chinese equivalents: `{节点} 市占率`, `{节点} 交期`, `{节点} BOM 成本占比`, `{公司} {节点} 客户 认证 产能`

If only C-grade sources appear, keep the node in watch mode and say what A/B source would change the conclusion.
