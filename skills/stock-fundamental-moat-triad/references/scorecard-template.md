# Scorecard Template

Use this reference only when the user asks for a ranking, comparison table, or repeatable scorecard.

## Gating Rules

Apply these gates before scoring. These gates calibrate confidence and cap the `current evidence verdict`; they do not replace the forward-looking judgment.

- If value-chain exposure is not proven, cap the current verdict at `concept-adjacent`.
- If same-node international peer benchmarking is missing, cap the current verdict at `qualified supplier with upside` and mark the peer field as `N/A`.
- If same-node international peers are clearly ahead on key specs and the target has no evidenced catch-up path, cap the current verdict at `option-like candidate`.
- If customer certification evidence is C-level or lower, cap the current verdict at `option-like candidate`.
- If the product is only an announced target with no customer/application proof, mark the future highlight as `watch only`.
- If current financial or market data is required but unavailable, score fundamentals only and mark valuation/trading fields as `N/A`.

Classify the forward-looking conclusion first:

- `strong future highlight`: material driver, meaningful earnings path, credible customer/product/catch-up milestones, and observable confirmation path.
- `medium future highlight`: real future driver, but value capture, peer gap, customer entry, or financial translation still needs proof.
- `weak future highlight`: plausible story, but earnings path is small, delayed, low-value, or mostly dependent on external narrative.
- `not visible`: no credible path from future demand to company earnings.

Then classify the future highlight evidence state:

- `clear future highlight`: material demand or product-generation driver, meaningful value capture, visible customer/product milestones, and partial or better source support.
- `early optionality`: plausible future route and meaningful upside, but proof is still early.
- `watch only`: interesting direction, but value capture, peer gap, customer path, or financial translation is unclear.
- `low future relevance`: no credible path from future demand to company earnings.

## Suggested 100-Point Rubric

| Dimension | Weight | What To Check |
|---|---:|---|
| Future demand and inflection | 25 | Future driver, demand timing, product-generation change, policy/localization window, cycle position |
| Product/node fit and value capture | 20 | Whether the company product sits in the future value node, expected ASP/mix/share change, bottleneck migration |
| Customer adoption path | 20 | Sample, validation, reliability, AVL/BOM, pilot, mass-production probability and timeline |
| International peer catch-up risk | 15 | Same-node leaders, technical/customer/manufacturing gap, route substitution, localization opening |
| Financial translation | 15 | Revenue path, gross margin, yield, utilization, operating leverage, capex/depreciation burden |
| Evidence and invalidation quality | 5 | Official support, contradiction risk, missing proof, next source to check |

## Score Interpretation

| Score | Future Conclusion | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 85-100 | strong future highlight | Material future driver with credible earnings path and visible milestones |
| 70-84 | medium future highlight | Future driver is real, but one major link still needs proof |
| 55-69 | weak future highlight | Plausible direction, but earnings path or timing is still uncertain |
| 40-54 | watch only | Interesting theme, but value capture/customer path is weak |
| <40 | not visible | No credible path from future demand to company earnings |

## Next-Signal Checklist

Track the next signal that can upgrade or downgrade the thesis:

- named customer or platform owner disclosure;
- AVL/BOM or design-in evidence;
- pilot/batch/mass-production wording change;
- segment revenue or high-margin product mix change;
- gross margin, capacity utilization, yield, or ASP trend;
- international peer product spec, price cut, design-in, or capacity expansion;
- technology route change that moves the value node.
