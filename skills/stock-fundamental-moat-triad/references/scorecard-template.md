# Scorecard Template

Use this reference only when the user asks for a ranking, comparison table, or repeatable scorecard.

## Gating Rules

Apply these gates before scoring:

- If value-chain exposure is not proven, cap the total verdict at `concept-adjacent`.
- If same-node international peer benchmarking is missing, cap the total verdict at `qualified supplier with upside` and mark the peer field as `N/A`.
- If same-node international peers are clearly ahead on key specs and the target has no evidenced catch-up path, cap the total verdict at `option-like candidate`.
- If customer certification evidence is C-level or lower, cap the total verdict at `option-like candidate`.
- If the product is only an announced target with no customer/application proof, mark `watch only`.
- If current financial or market data is required but unavailable, score fundamentals only and mark valuation/trading fields as `N/A`.

## Suggested 100-Point Rubric

| Dimension | Weight | What To Check |
|---|---:|---|
| Value-chain position | 20 | Exact node, value share, bottleneck status, downstream pull, revenue directness |
| International peer benchmark | 20 | Same-node global leaders, technical parameters, BOM/customer proof, manufacturing moat, route risk |
| Porter Five Forces | 20 | Buyer power, supplier power, rivalry, substitutes, entry barriers, pricing power |
| Customer certification barrier | 25 | Validation stage, customer proof, switching cost, qualification cycle, repeat orders |
| Financial translation | 10 | Segment revenue, gross margin, capacity utilization, cash conversion, operating leverage |
| Evidence quality | 5 | Official filing, named customer, hard contract/order, contradiction risk, disclosure clarity |

## Score Interpretation

| Score | Verdict | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 85-100 | core beneficiary | High-quality node, near-parity or leading international benchmark, good competitive structure, and A/B customer evidence |
| 70-84 | qualified supplier with upside | Real exposure and plausible moat, but peer gap, customer proof, or financial translation is incomplete |
| 55-69 | option-like candidate | Product direction is plausible; commercialization evidence is early |
| 40-54 | concept-adjacent | Theme relation exists, but value-chain or certification proof is weak |
| <40 | reject | Claimed exposure is unsupported or commercially immaterial |

## Next-Signal Checklist

Track the next signal that can upgrade or downgrade the thesis:

- named customer or platform owner disclosure;
- AVL/BOM or design-in evidence;
- pilot/batch/mass-production wording change;
- segment revenue or high-margin product mix change;
- gross margin, capacity utilization, yield, or ASP trend;
- international peer product spec, price cut, design-in, or capacity expansion;
- technology route change that moves the value node.
